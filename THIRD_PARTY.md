# Third-party materials

The MIT grant covers the original study code, synthetic public controls and
original writing. It does not relicense historical CA certificates, CRLs,
operator-supplied signed payloads, public incident metadata or third-party
material quoted or referenced in the reports and manifests.

Historical files in `benchmark/artifacts/` and source metadata retain their
original provenance and any applicable publisher terms. `benchmark/cases.json`
labels each case as historical or synthetic. `provenance.json` and `SOURCES.md`
identify the sources and original investigators. No third-party endorsement is
implied.

All included signing controls were generated locally for the experiment; their
private keys are not included. The historical issuer certificates contain only
public keys. This repository contains no private-key recovery work.
